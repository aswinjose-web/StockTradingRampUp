# Learning Academy inventory

36 original lessons across twelve progressive levels. Each includes meaning, purpose, analogy, worked example, formula/process, visual explanation, editable demonstration, practical exercise, common mistake, research application, knowledge check and an external authoritative reference. Quizzes and bookmarks persist locally; completion requires a correct answer.

This is a complete introductory survey rather than an exhaustive course on every subtopic. Advanced strategy backtesting, detailed derivatives models and tax computations are documented roadmap items. All numeric company examples are synthetic.

## Accuracy conventions

Formulas are tested in tests/engines.test.ts. SIP uses month-end contributions and effective annual-to-monthly conversion. Financial imports use unscaled rupees and share counts. Average-equity ROE and weighted-average EPS conventions are explained; workbench simplifications are explicitly labelled. Indicators use past/current data only, warm-up suppression and documented smoothing. Time-sensitive rates and settlement/contract details are directed to current official sources; reference review date is 10 October 2026.
